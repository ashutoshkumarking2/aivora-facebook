Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect essential information required to deliver an uncompromising intelligence platform. This includes:

  • Account Details: Email addresses and display names provided directly or through third-party authentication services.
  • Authentication Identifiers: Data provided via Google or Facebook OAuth (such as UID, public profile picture, and email).
  • Usage Technical Information: IP address, browser type, and interaction cookies managed safely via Firebase SDK.

2. Authentication & Data Sharing

Authentication is handled securely via Firebase Authentication. Utilizing Facebook Login or Google Sign-In grants authentication rights only and does not grant Aivora AI access to private social communications, friend lists, or timeline publishing permissions.

3. Third-Party Infrastructure

Aivora AI utilizes Google Cloud Firebase to handle account state, security tokenization, and infrastructure management. Data is governed in alignment with standard enterprise privacy requirements.

4. User Rights and Data Removal

Users maintain complete rights over their personal information. To submit a data deletion request, please navigate to our User Data Deletion Request Page.

5. Contact Information

For questions regarding this privacy policy, contact our compliance team at privacy@aivora.ai.
